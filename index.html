Traveler Cheat-Sheet

  • 🛂
    Visa: Apply for the official online ETA before boarding your flight.
  • 🔌
    Power Plug: Type D, G (UK standard 3-pin) and M. 230V / 50Hz.
  • 📱
    SIM Card: Pick up a Dialog or Mobitel tourist eSIM/SIM at Colombo airport arrival.
  • 🛺
    Taxis: Use the PickMe or Uber apps in Colombo & Kandy for metered tuk-tuks.
  • 🙏
    Temple Etiquette: Cover shoulders and knees; remove shoes and hats before entering.
