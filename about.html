My Story & Mission

Growing up in Sri Lanka, I spent my childhood exploring ancient ruins in Anuradhapura, listening to the rhythmic chants of temple drummers in Kandy, and watching wild elephants roam freely through national parks.

Over the years, as international travelers increasingly discovered Sri Lanka, I noticed many were overwhelmed by confusing train booking systems, inaccurate foreign exchange guidelines, or aggressive tourist traps. I created this blog to be a clear, transparent beacon for anyone visiting our pearl of an island.

"My mission is simple: to make sure you experience the warmth of Sri Lankan hospitality, enjoy ethical wildlife adventures, and leave our shores with lifelong memories."

What I Focus On

1. Honest Route Itineraries: Whether you have 7, 10, or 14 days, I curate balanced routes connecting iconic highlights (like Sigiriya and Ella) with off-the-beaten-path villages.

2. Cultural Sensitivity: Practical advice on sacred temple etiquette, traditional dress codes, and respectful photography around monks and Buddhist shrines.

3. Fair Pricing & Scams Awareness: Accurate ticket rates for foreigners, metered tuk-tuk apps, and reliable train timetable guidance so you travel stress-free.
